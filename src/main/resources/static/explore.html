Heads up: embeddings are not trained. Scores demonstrate pipeline mechanics, not semantic quality.

Tessera is byte-level (so alice ≠ Alice) and Mosaic vectors are random per token. A sentence's score is the mean of all its token vectors — long sentences average toward zero, so a sentence that literally contains your query word can still lose to an unrelated shorter one by sheer luck of the seed. This is not substring search.

    Tessera works on raw UTF-8 bytes. Each token shows its decoded text and numeric ID. Whitespace, punctuation, and case all produce distinct tokens.

    Scores rise when both texts share BPE tokens. With random embeddings, identical substrings drive similarity — semantic kinship does not. Try rephrasing while keeping shared vocabulary.
